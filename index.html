Our Story

A dark room, a hot grill, one obsession.

NOIRGRILL started as a two-person night counter with a single grill and a rule nobody was allowed to break: if a layer doesn't change the bite, it doesn't go on the burger.

Six years on, the room is bigger and the lights are still low — but the rule runs the kitchen. We grind chuck every morning, bake brioche twice a day, and brine our pickles in-house. 617 is the number on our first-ever ticket. It's still the standard every plate is judged against.

The Craft

Every layer has a job.

The same five disciplines behind the burger you just watched build itself.

01The Bun

Brioche baked twice daily, toasted in rendered fat for a crust that holds the stack together.

02The Fire

Chuck ground coarse every morning, smashed thin over open flame for maximum char.

03The Cheese

Aged cheddar, sliced thick, steamed under a cloche until it runs to every corner.

04The Tomato

Vine tomatoes salted ten minutes early, so they season the bite instead of watering it.

05The Greens

Iceberg and garden leaves, cut thick and kept cold — the crunch is structural.
